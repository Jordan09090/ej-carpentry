EJ Carpentry & Maintenance handles carpentry repairs and property maintenance across Perth Metro — from sticky doors and swelling timber to strata common-area upkeep and insurance repair work. We work efficiently, communicate clearly, and leave every site tidy.

Keep Your Property Safe, Functional & Looking Sharp

Doors that stick, timber that's swelled with the seasons, worn hardware, minor reinstatements after damage — this is the day-to-day carpentry work that keeps a property running smoothly. We handle planned upkeep and on-the-spot fixes with clear communication and tidy work practices.

We work with homeowners, strata schemes, landlords and small businesses, adjusting to whichever kind of property we're on — from a single house call to recurring maintenance across shared common areas.

Property Maintenance FAQs

Do you handle insurance repair work?

Yes — we carry out carpentry repairs for insurance claims, including minor structural reinstatement. We can provide itemised quotes suited to the claims process.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance for strata or rental properties?

Yes — we work with strata managers and landlords on both one-off repairs and ongoing maintenance arrangements, with clear communication suited to shared and tenanted properties.

Can you fix a sticky door or swelling timber?

Yes — sticky doors, swelling timber and minor hardware issues are some of the most common maintenance calls we get, and are usually quick, straightforward fixes.

Do you take on small, one-off jobs or only larger projects?

We take on both — a single repair is just as welcome as a larger maintenance job. Let us know what needs doing and we'll quote it accordingly.

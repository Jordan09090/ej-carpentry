Frequently Asked Questions

How much does carpentry work cost in Perth?

Every job is different, so we quote based on scope, materials and access. Send us photos and a brief description of the work and we'll come back with a fast, itemised, no-obligation quote.

What suburbs and areas do you service?

We service the greater Perth Metro area, including surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether your location is covered, just ask when you request a quote.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, EJ Carpentry & Maintenance is fully insured. We work to a high standard on both residential and strata/commercial jobs.

How quickly can you start my job?

Turnaround depends on current bookings and the size of the job, but we aim to respond to every enquiry quickly and give you a realistic start date up front.

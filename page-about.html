About Us

Professional Carpentry & Maintenance in Perth, WA

EJ Carpentry & Maintenance is run by Ezra, a carpenter who’s had a love for timber and good design since he was a kid. Inspired by his dad, he started his apprenticeship at 16, working on luxury new builds and all kinds of residential and commercial maintenance. Now, he’s gone out on his own—bringing quality craftsmanship, an eye for detail, and a genuine passion for the trade to every job.

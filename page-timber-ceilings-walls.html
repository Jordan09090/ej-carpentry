Warm Up Your Space With Natural Timber

From battened feature walls to patio and alfresco ceiling linings, timber adds warmth and texture that flat plaster can't match. We custom-fit every job to the space — cutting cleanly around lighting, air vents and existing architecture rather than working around them awkwardly.

Finishes range from natural oils that showcase the grain through to durable clear coats built to handle outdoor exposure, so the finish suits both the look you want and where it's installed.

Timber Ceilings & Walls FAQs

What's the difference between wall battens and slat cladding?

Battens are individual timber strips fixed with gaps between them, often used to create a feature wall or acoustic effect. Slat cladding is usually a pre-assembled panel system. We can advise which suits your space and budget.

Do timber battens work in outdoor alfresco areas?

Yes — alfresco and patio ceiling linings are one of our most common timber ceiling jobs. We select timber and finishes suited to outdoor exposure so it holds up to Perth's sun and weather.

Can you work around existing lighting and air-conditioning?

Yes — we coordinate with electricians and air-conditioning placement so battens, slats and cladding line up cleanly around fixtures rather than being cut around awkwardly.

How long does a feature timber wall or ceiling take?

A single feature wall typically takes one to two days; a full ceiling lining depends on the area and detailing involved. We'll give you a clear timeframe as part of your quote.
